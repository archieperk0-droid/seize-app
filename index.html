Phone Reminders
How to get reminders on your phone
This app runs in your browser, which cannot directly push native phone notifications. Here are the best methods to bridge that gap:
Option A — Browser Push Notifications (Recommended)
  1. Click "Enable Push Notifications" below — your browser will ask for permission.
  2. Add reminders in the panel below with a time and message.
  3. Keep the browser tab open (or pinned) — notifications fire even when the tab is in background.
  4. On iPhone: Add this page to Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) for app-like behaviour.
Option B — Calendar Export
  1. Click "Export to .ics" to download your timetable as a calendar file.
  2. Open the .ics file on your phone — it will import into Apple Calendar / Google Calendar.
  3. Your phone's calendar app handles all notifications automatically.
